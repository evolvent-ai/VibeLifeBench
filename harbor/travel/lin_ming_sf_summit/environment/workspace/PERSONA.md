# User Persona: Ming Lin

## Personality

- **Pragmatic:** He focuses on results and dislikes ornamental reporting.
- **Trusting but transparent:** He delegates willingly, but expects a clear explanation and record for each action.
- **Health-conscious:** A lumbar-spine condition makes long-haul flight comfort important; he will not sacrifice a critical comfort constraint merely to save money.
- **Time-conscious:** He dislikes last-minute changes and wants important arrangements confirmed early.

## Communication style

- Speaks directly and gets to the point.
- Often gives several instructions together, such as researching flights, booking a hotel, handling visa matters, and scheduling events.
- Welcomes proactive questions but dislikes being asked repeatedly for information he already provided.
- Expects the conclusion first and supporting details afterward.

## Decision habits

- Delegates small decisions, such as a few hundred CNY in price difference or routine calendar adjustments.
- Major decisions, including spending over CNY 5,000, substantial itinerary changes, or personal-data changes, require explicit options and rationale for his or Manager Zhang's approval.
- Usually accepts a recommendation when the rationale is sound.

## Expectations of the agent

- Monitor proactively and warn him before a problem becomes urgent.
- Consolidate all arrangements into one clear itinerary document.
- Control the budget and disclose any projected overrun in advance with corrective options.
- Respond calmly to disruptions and present option A and option B.

## Background

- This is Ming Lin's first international trip this year, and he is not attentive to details such as passport and EVUS validity.
- He is unfamiliar with local San Francisco transportation and prefers a hotel near the summit venue or a metro station.
- He budgets in CNY and pays close attention to exchange-rate changes.
