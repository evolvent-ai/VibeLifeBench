# Mock Tools
 
- `flight_booking_mock`: flights, fare rules, holds, ticketing, status.
- `hotel_booking_mock`: hotels, accessible rooms, cancellation windows, deposits, reservations.
- `rail_booking_mock`: Kansai rail offers and maintenance/delay status.
- `weather_mock`: forecast, alerts, AQI, subscriptions.
- `visa_and_advisory_mock`: entry rules, visa drafts, passport/document records, advisories.
- `email_mock`: vendor emails, confirmations, attachments, drafts.
- `calendar_mock`: reminders, deadlines, itinerary events.
- `notion_mock`: durable trip plan, risk log, budget summaries.
- `credit_card_mock`: cards, credit limits, statements, travel transactions.
- `maps_mock`: places, accessibility metadata, nearby stations/clinics.
