[2026-05-29 08:00 +08:00] WHO travel advisory update: Indonesia, including Bali, Lombok, and Java, remains an active Zika monitoring zone. Aedes mosquito activity is elevated during June through August. WHO recommends that pregnant travelers consult their healthcare provider before traveling to affected areas because infection during pregnancy can cause microcephaly and other birth defects. Strongly use personal protection such as repellent, long sleeves, and air-conditioned accommodation for all travelers.
