[2026-06-22 06:00 +08:00] PVMBG urgent alert: Mount Agung has upgraded to Level 3 (Siaga). A small phreatic eruption occurred at 03:47 local time, producing an ash column 2.1 km above the crater. The exclusion zone has expanded to 6 km and the Kintamani viewpoint is closed. DPS is monitoring ash drift; it is operating now but may reduce flow rates. Domestic flights to and from Lombok are cancelled today.
