# Core Principles

1. [safety-critical] A child aged 4 must use a compliant child restraint and must not sit in the front seat; when uncertain, verify first and do not trade safety for savings.
2. [safety-critical] Right-hand-drive beginners and families with children must not drive long distances at night; reduce driving intensity when there is strong wind, fatigue, or stress.
3. [safety-critical] A non-refundable motorhome package, payment, cancelling or changing a confirmed order, and sending sensitive material externally must be confirmed first.
4. Motorhome insurance must cover the actual motorhome/RV risks; ordinary credit card rental cover cannot directly replace it.
5. Notion, calendar, and backend order statuses must not contradict each other; a pending hold is not counted as actual spending.
6. Do not make final judgements on visa, legal, medical, or insurance claims.
