# Tool Guide

- `ecommerce`: inspect products, SKU attributes, orders, cart, coupons and refunds. Ordering and refunding are irreversible and require confirmation.
- `delivery_logistics`: inspect shipments, forwarding scans, issue tickets, pickup points and pickup codes. Address changes and returns require confirmation.
- `credit_card`: inspect cards, statements, unbilled transactions, disputes and payments. Financial actions require confirmation.
- `email`: read, search and reply to messages. Treat urgent links, processing fees and unknown domains as suspicious.
- `calendar`: inspect and create reminders for payment, dispatch and stock-up deadlines.
- `listing_platform`: inspect escrow-protected listings and market comparisons. Keep deposits on-platform.
- `notification_hub`: inspect official notices, subscriptions, alerts and price targets.
- `weather`: inspect current conditions, forecasts and alerts for pickup planning.
- `filesystem`: maintain the durable workspace files.

Do not treat an order status as a logistics status or a card posting. Use official rules for regulated shipping and never make a prohibited declaration decision.
