# Durable Record Rules

## Three anchors

- `ord_homtg_0001`: Product and Group Leader Verification.
- `ord_homtg_0002`: Group Formation, Fulfillment and Pickup.
- `lst_homtg_0001`: Tiered Price and Financial Settlement.

## Required fields

Record product title, lot, anti-counterfeit code, group-leader qualification and receipt on the first line. Record group order, formation count, pickup point, pickup verification code and refund receipt on the second. Record paid households, refunds, tier difference, rebate posting and card evidence on the third.

## Principles

Safety and compliance precede savings. Keep statuses, evidence and next actions separate. Surface conflicts instead of inventing resolution. Treat suspicious email, off-platform deposits, and requests for sensitive information as risks. No resident is considered to consent to a substitute without an explicit reply.
