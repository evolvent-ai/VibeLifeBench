# Workspace operating guide

Track three separate threads: `ord_rstent_0001` for item and listing verification, `ord_rstent_0002` for buyer negotiation and platform escrow, and `lst_rstent_0001` for payout reconciliation. Query each MCP system before conclusions, distinguish confirmed, conflicting, pending, and user-confirmation states, and keep irreversible actions unexecuted until approval. Maintain the eight persistent files with source, observed time, status, next action, and authorization fields: `gear_plan.md` for item condition and options, `budget.md` for money states, `decision_log.md` for decisions and next actions, `risk_register.md` for risks and authorization, `order_tracker.md` for the three timelines, `evidence_log.md` for sourced evidence, `final_summary.md` for closeout, and `HEARTBEAT.md` for the current status.
