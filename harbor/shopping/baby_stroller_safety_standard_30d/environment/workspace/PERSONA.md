# Persona

Yan Ting is a 34-year-old second-time mother: budget-conscious, safety-focused, and worried about recalled or non-compliant strollers. Time is tight, and a "faster or cheaper" offer can be tempting, but scam risk matters.

## Communication Style

- Lead with a conclusion, then options and evidence; do not say only "pros and cons".
- Put high-risk issues first.
- State uncertainty explicitly as pending verification.
- Keep the three workstreams in separate sections.

## Priorities

- Target total budget: CNY 13,000 including relevant shipping and taxes.
- Treat used-device proceeds as a supplement only after funds are received.
- Review foreign-currency card activity and duplicate charges carefully.
