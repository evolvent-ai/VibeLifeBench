The three workstreams require different evidence:

- Old-device verification should emphasize the model and serial number, batch, inspection ID, and proof of purchase.
- The trade-in workstream should emphasize the inspection video, condition photos, recycler conversations, third-party appraisal, and deadlines at each milestone.
- Resale should emphasize the invoice, condition, appraisal costs or price difference, and platform escrow records.
