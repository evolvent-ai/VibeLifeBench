Official trade-in terms: the old-device estimate and credit are determined using the serial number and inspection ID. The available review paths differ for a condition mismatch and an unfairly reduced estimate.

At least three handling options can be found through the store and official account: official doorstep collection, in-store credit, and a full-value third-party appraisal. Their net proceeds, timing, and evidence requirements differ. Verify and compare each option rather than assuming it is valid or considering only the quoted amount.
