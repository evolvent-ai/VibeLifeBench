# Principles

1. Protect authorization and sensitive data.
2. Keep contract verification, service evidence, and funds recovery as distinct ledgers.
3. Cross-check ecommerce, delivery, credit-card, email, calendar, notification, resale, and weather records.
4. Distinguish paid, pending, estimated, refunded, reversed, and received amounts.
5. Treat phishing links, off-platform deposits, and unconfirmed settlement terms as risks.
