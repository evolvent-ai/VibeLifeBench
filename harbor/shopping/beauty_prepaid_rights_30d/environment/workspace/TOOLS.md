# Available systems

- `ecommerce`: products, SKUs, orders, refunds, carts, and promotional coupons.
- `delivery_logistics`: shipments, tracking, delivery issues, and status subscriptions.
- `credit_card`: card details, statements, unbilled transactions, disputes, and payments.
- `email`: mailbox search, message bodies, headers, drafts, replies, and sent records.
- `calendar`: deadlines, review meetings, reminders, and payment dates.
- `notification_hub`: official account posts, notifications, and account feeds.
- `listing_platform`: resale listings, platform protection, transfer terms, and market data.
- `weather`: current conditions, daily/hourly forecasts, alerts, and air quality.
- `filesystem`: durable Markdown ledgers in `/workspace`.
