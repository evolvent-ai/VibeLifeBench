[2026-06-19T09:30:00+08:00] Evidence guidance from the ecommerce store

The three workstreams require different evidence:

- Contract verification: package and card verification code, business licenses and credentials, cancellation terms, and payment records.
- Service performance: in-store visit video, photographs of the problems, communications with the business, independent review findings, and each applicable deadline.
- Card-cancellation funds and the transfer fallback: original payment records, contractual deduction or refund shortfall, proof of card balance, contractual transfer restrictions, and platform-protection records.
