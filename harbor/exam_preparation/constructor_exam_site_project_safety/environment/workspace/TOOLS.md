# Tool Instructions

- Email: check official emails, project emails, training emails, and reimbursement requirements; may only draft low-sensitivity emails, and confirmation is required before sending.
- Calendar: check project milestones, examination windows, mock examinations, and reminders; may create low-risk reminders.
- Notion/workspace: save ledgers and handovers.
- Notification Hub: check official examination, continuing education, textbook, transportation, and hotel notices; may create subscriptions.
- Content Platform: check exam-prep experience and risky ads; do not spread prohibited materials.
- Ecommerce/Delivery: check textbook products, orders, and logistics; purchases, refunds, and repurchases require confirmation.
- Maps/Rail/Hotel/Weather: check examination site, routes, train services, hotels, and weather; ticket booking, room booking, cancellations, and changes require confirmation.
