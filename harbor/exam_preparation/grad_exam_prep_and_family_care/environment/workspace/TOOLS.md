# Tool Conventions
- calendar_mock: study blocks, mocks, clinic follow-ups, and exam reminders.
- notion_mock: exam Control Center, scores, completion, budget, and health summaries.
- ecommerce_mock: study aids, stationery, cold-compress gel, and ice packs.
- banking_mock: budget account and transaction reconciliation.
- email_mock: Dr. Wang messages, exam seating, and syllabus notices.
- notification_hub_mock: stock, progress, health, email, and closeout subscriptions.
- health_tracker_mock: postoperative readings. Pain descriptions are stored in `heart_rate.value_text` and exposed by `list_health_alerts`.
