# notion environment: campus_ai_infra_offer_deadline_tradeoff

This environment describes the daily business status that Lin Che can see through the notion service during the spring 2026 period when graduation and campus recruitment run in parallel.

- Stage 0 only includes historical records, current status, and known schedule that already existed or had already been determined as of 2026-05-12.
- Subsequent offers, deadline changes, interview progress, and new posts on the content platform are written into the corresponding Stage via silent mutation in `event.yaml`.
- People, companies, and business flows are synthetic; publicly available facts related to LLM serving, CUDA, and personal information protection are retained in a reproducible record in the external source ledger.
- Records are organized by natural search, temporal relationships, and cross-service object associations, without using answer hint fields or grading-specific naming.
