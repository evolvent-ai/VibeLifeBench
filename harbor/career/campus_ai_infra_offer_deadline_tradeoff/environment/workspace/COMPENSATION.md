
# COMPENSATION

- Annual cash package = monthly base * number of salaries + cash bonus confirmed in writing + subsidies confirmed in writing.
- RSUs, options, unvested equity, verbal bonuses, and amounts pending approval must all be listed separately.
- 420,000 is the pre-tax cash bottom line and should not be disclosed externally as the negotiation floor.
- External communication may ask about salary structure, payment conditions, onboarding time, and deadline, but must not disclose the internal comparison table.
