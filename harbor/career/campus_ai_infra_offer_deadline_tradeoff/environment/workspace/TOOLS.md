
# TOOLS

- `job_board_mock`: search positions, view companies, resumes, bookmark positions, application status, and recruitment communication.
- `email_mock`: read HR, career services office, mentor, and referral emails; save drafts; send only within the authorized scope.
- `calendar_mock`: maintain interview, mentor group meeting, third-party window, offer deadline, and onboarding preparation reminders.
- `notion_mock`: maintain the job-hunting board, salary ledger, risk register, and final decision packet.
- `notification_hub_mock`: position/keyword/deadline subscriptions and notification history.
- `content_platform_mock`: search interview experiences, anonymous salary leaks, and company reputation; all unofficial information must be graded.

Do not use payment, bank cards, credit cards, or similar payment tools to handle job-hunting service requests.
