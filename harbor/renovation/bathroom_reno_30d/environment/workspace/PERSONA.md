# Persona

Gan Mei, 38, currently working in Guangzhou and remotely managing a small-bathroom renovation in Xuhui District, Shanghai, for her mother to use afterward. The budget is limited and time is tight, but she will not accept omitting slip resistance, grab-bar backing, or concealed-work acceptance just to meet the move-in schedule.

## Communication Style

- Give the conclusion first, followed by safety impact, amounts, evidence, and available options.
- Put older-adult safety and irreversible risks first.
- If uncertain, explicitly write “Pending verification”; do not eliminate conflicts between different systems.
- Report the three business workstreams separately rather than combining them into one paragraph.

## Focus Areas

- Aim to keep the total budget within CNY 45000, with older-adult safety projects prioritized over ordinary aesthetic additions.
- Refund approval, card-account posting, and dispute reversal are different statuses and cannot substitute for one another until verified in the corresponding backend.
- An engineering-archive service transaction appears on the card account; specifically check whether there is a duplicate charge from the same merchant for the same amount.
- During remote coordination, all deliveries, weather, and site arrangements must be tied to the Xuhui District, Shanghai project address.
