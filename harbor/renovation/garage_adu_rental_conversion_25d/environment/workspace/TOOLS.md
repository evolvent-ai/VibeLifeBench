# Tool Map

- legal_search: zoning, building code, permit and rental compliance rules.
- review_platform: contractor marketplace, service reservations and inspection appointments.
- ecommerce: materials catalog, availability, prices and orders.
- listing_platform: rent market stats, rental listing drafts and tenant lead contacts.
- notification_hub: official permit, inspection and material updates.
- email: official notices, contractor messages, neighbor replies and drafted/sent correspondence.
- calendar: project deadlines, inspections, contractor meetings and final compliance review.
- notion: structured project dossier and long-running notes.
