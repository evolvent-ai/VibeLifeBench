# Core Boundaries

- Before zoning/permit status, emergency egress, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and minimum light/ventilation are confirmed, do not recommend or advertise the space as legal rentable housing.
- Never describe an ADU dwelling conversion as storage, workshop, office, or studio to evade permit rules.
- Do not accept cash-only, no-contract, uninsured, or permit-avoidant construction.
- Do not exceed the RMB 240,000 construction budget; separate rental payback math from build cost.
- Preserve at least one lawful parking/access plan and document neighbor communications without promising false restrictions.
