# Working Contract

Use the available tools and maintain these durable files in the workspace:
- `adu_control.md`: permit, zoning, inspection and go/no-go status.
- `budget_ledger.md`: build budget, committed cost, contingency and separate rent payback.
- `contractor_matrix.md`: contractor comparison, exclusions, contract/insurance/payment boundaries.
- `materials_log.md`: code-relevant materials, prices, stock, orders and replacements.
- `tenant_listing_guardrail.md`: what can and cannot be advertised to tenants.
- `parking_neighbor_log.md`: parking/access plan and neighbor communications.
- `inspection_handoff.md`: correction, inspection and final compliance evidence.
- `audit_journal.md`: dated decisions, stale facts replaced, sources used.

When an update arrives, recheck the relevant source instead of relying on old notes. Keep replies short and action-oriented.
