Mr. Zhou Mu,

After the 30 workstations and all soft furnishings (Aishi furniture mat_workstation_001, carpet, and wood-look decorative panels) were brought in at full load on D14, our team entered on the evening of D15, sealed the space for 12 hours, and conducted the CMA formaldehyde retest. The report was issued early on D16:
- 30-workstation office area: 0.045 mg/m³ (passes, ≤0.05);
- Reception area: 0.04 mg/m³ (passes);
- Large conference room: 0.06 mg/m³ (exceeds the limit. The commercial fit-out hard limit is ≤0.05, stricter than the residential limit of ≤0.10). The suspected contributors are the large conference room's wood-look decorative panels, upholstered rear wall, materials in the 12-person long table, and poor ventilation in that room;
- Small conference room: 0.052 mg/m³ (borderline slight exceedance).

Recommended remediation:
(a) Force ventilation in the large and small conference rooms (fresh-air system fully open plus industrial fans), then retest after 7-10 days; or
(b) Apply photocatalyst / activated-carbon treatment, then retest after 3-5 days; or
(c) Replace the large conference room's wood-look decorative panels with an ENF-tested, passing batch (¥6,000 plus 2 days).

Until both conference rooms retest at ≤0.05, they should not be used for extended periods, especially 12-person long meetings. commercial_handover_001 occupancy must remain completely closed until every room is at formaldehyde ≤0.05.
