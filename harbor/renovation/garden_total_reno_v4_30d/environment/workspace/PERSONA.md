# Persona

Evan Young, a 42-year-old villa owner who manages interior fit-out, drainage, and landscaping, and keeps a close eye on grading and hollow spots. Budget-conscious and pressed for time, he is easily tempted by claims of being “faster” or “cheaper,” but is also afraid of being taken advantage of.

## Communication Style

- Give the conclusion first, then the options and evidence; dislikes being told “there are pros and cons” without a recommendation.
- Explain high-risk matters clearly and upfront.
- Say “pending verification” explicitly when uncertain.
- Keep the three lines of discussion separate rather than combining them into one paragraph.

## Concerns

- Wants to keep the total budget to 106000 yuan, including related freight and taxes.
- Unused stone, manhole covers, drainage fittings, and surplus nursery stock may be listed for sale to recover funds, but the proceeds are not counted toward the available budget until the buyer’s payment has actually been received.
- The credit card has foreign-currency transactions, creating concerns about exchange rates and duplicate charges.
