# Persona

Name: Chen Yu (Chen Yu).

Role: Owner of a 68 sqm two-bedroom apartment in Shanghai Pudong. Renovating
in preparation for a fixed family move-in. Works full time at a tech firm in
Shanghai; her partner also works full time. They have a 4-year-old child.
Chen Yu's parents occasionally visit and stay overnight, so accessibility in
the main bathroom matters.

Communication style:

- Direct, time-constrained. Prefers bullets with timestamps over prose.
- Comfortable in Mandarin and English — match her opening language.
- Wants decisions surfaced as options, not open-ended questions ("A or B").
- Expects risks called out before they become visible problems.
- Will accept "I don't know yet, will verify with X" — refuses speculation
  dressed up as confidence.
- Has limited evening / weekend availability; tries to keep weekday daytime
  for site visits when needed.

Authorization scope:

- Can approve quotes and tentative vendor holds within the hard budget cap.
- Can authorize change orders and material substitutions if the budget
  impact and downstream risk are spelled out.
- Will not sign off on construction phases that bypass an inspection gate or
  a property filing in pending/RFI/rejected state.
- Will not release final payment with open punch-list defects or unresolved
  property paperwork.

What she cares about:

- Whether the apartment is genuinely move-in-ready by the family deadline,
  not just "construction done" on paper.
- Indoor air quality before the child moves in (low-VOC paint, adequate
  ventilation window after finishes).
- Bathroom safety for occasional senior visitors (slip resistance, dry/wet
  separation).
- Predictable cash flow — no surprise change-order signatures that consume
  the reserve without explanation.
- Neighbor relations — does not want construction noise to generate property
  warnings or escalations.

What she does not want:

- A "polished plan" that quietly leaves waterproofing, electrical
  acceptance, or property filing unresolved.
- Generic to-do lists without owner, status, and next action per line.
- Final-payment release recommendation when any gate is still open.
- Updates that ignore changes you should have caught by polling tools.
