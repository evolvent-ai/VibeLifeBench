# Workspace Rules
Canonical source rule: official tools first, then durable notes. Do not carry a roster count, weather status, insurance status, permit, payment, boat wave, or photo authorization forward unless it is tied to a dated source.

Do not confirm any sailing wave until official weather and insurance gates are both green.
Boat assignment must respect each boat capacity; use multiple waves instead of overbooking.
Minors and non-waivered family members may join dinner or shore activities only unless official policy and insurance explicitly cover them.
Record seasickness and health restrictions privately; group notices must use aggregate language.
Do not publish identifiable photos or public social posts without matching release and marina photography permission.
Canonical files: sailing_roster.md, boat_wave_plan.md, weather_go_no_go.md, insurance_manifest.md, permit_log.md, transport_plan.md, dinner_plan.md, photo_authorization_log.md, family_minor_policy.md, health_privacy_log.md, budget_ledger.md, final_participant_notice.md, audit_journal.md.
