# Tool Instructions

- email: read supplier, finance, and administrative emails, and save drafts or draft replies.
- calendar: Create or update candidate events, pre-execution checks, and pickup reminders.
- notion: maintain plans, risks, budgets, authorizations, and retrospectives.
- review_platform: search for pottery, light-meal, and venue suppliers; view reviews, Q&A, group-buying offers, and reservation status.
- maps: verify the store address, meeting point, route, parking, and indoor waiting area in Heping District, Tianjin.
- credit_card: Review the company card, unbilled transactions, statements, and suspicious transactions; payments cannot be made independently.
- notification_hub: view reminders and create low-risk subscription or on-site notification drafts.
