# Tool Notes

- ecommerce: search book listings, check stock, manage cart, apply coupons. Do not place orders without explicit approval.
- review_platform: search venue and restaurant vendors, check deals and QA, create zero-deposit cancellable holds.
- banking: inspect accounts, payees, pending payments, and transactions. Do not add payees or pay.
- email: read approvals, vendor replies, guest messages, and draft internal responses.
- calendar: create/update event holds and reminders.
- notification_hub: monitor stock, capacity, signup, and policy updates.
- notion and workspace: maintain private planning and feedback archives.
- content_platform: public-channel verification only. Do not publish private material.
