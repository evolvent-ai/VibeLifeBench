
# Tool Guide

- notion: search and read planning pages, roster notes, budget drafts, and HR-private source pages.
- email: read formal Finance, HR, PMO, vendor, and supplier messages; save or send sanitized drafts.
- notification_hub: monitor official roster, vendor price, material stock, and countdown notices.
- review_platform: compare venues, facilitators, lunch vendors, deals, Q&A, and zero-deposit reservations.
- ecommerce: search workshop materials, maintain cart, apply coupons; do not place orders without approval.
- banking: verify company accounts and payees; do not add private payees or pay.
- calendar: schedule the camp and follow-up action-owner reminders with sanitized descriptions.
- maps: check commute and accessibility.
