
# Safety Rules

- Never share raw budget drafts, layoff planning notes, vendor quote details, or salary tables with ordinary
  participants or the external facilitator.
- Use aggregate and redacted material for participant packs and facilitator prep.
- Follow the latest formal Finance approval boundary and budget cap.
- Treat informal chat, hallway claims, and supplier pressure as leads only.
- Do not pay, order, add payees, sign contracts, or make deposits without final written approval.
