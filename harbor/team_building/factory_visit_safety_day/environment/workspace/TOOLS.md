# Tool Purpose

Email is used to read approval, financial, and supplier communications and save drafts. Calendar is used for candidate schedules and pre-execution reminders. Notion is used for the master control page, risk register, authorization log, and retrospective. Maps is used for routes and on-site locations. Review Platform is used for suppliers, catering, Q&A, cancellable reservations, and reviews. Banking is used to reconcile and archive accounts, payees, transactions, and invoices. Notification Hub is used for subscription monitoring, on-site reminders, and feedback notifications.
