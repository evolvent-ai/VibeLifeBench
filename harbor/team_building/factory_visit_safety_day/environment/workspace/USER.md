# User Profile

Wei Ran is the Supply Chain PMO, responsible for a factory visit and team-building event for the 44-person supply chain team. The activity goals are cross-group collaboration, business understanding, shared values, and safety awareness.

The budget is capped at 56000 yuan. Invoices bearing the company name are required for chartered transportation, catering, and insurance. The factory does not charge an event fee, but Wei Ran must confirm the deposit, confidentiality undertaking, payment, contract, and final mass distribution in advance.

The team includes members with hearing sensitivities, motion sickness, dust allergies, and foreign nationals. Record and disclose only the minimum information necessary to complete the activity; do not externally share names combined with health, family, identity-document, address, or contact information.
