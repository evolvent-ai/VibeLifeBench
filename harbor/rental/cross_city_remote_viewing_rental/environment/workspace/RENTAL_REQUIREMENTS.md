# RENTAL_REQUIREMENTS

Must-have:
- One-bedroom apartment.
- Monthly rent no more than 6,000 CNY.
- Peak door-to-door commute no more than 45 minutes.
- Move-in at least 2 days before the employment start date.
- Formal residence registration supported, with the residence-registration term verified in writing.

Nice-to-have: complete remote-video evidence, itemized fees, a reliable property-management record, and a practical moving handoff.

Deal-breakers: private payment, unclear authorization, unclear fees, an undocumented registration promise, missing verification for a key term, or pressure to act.
