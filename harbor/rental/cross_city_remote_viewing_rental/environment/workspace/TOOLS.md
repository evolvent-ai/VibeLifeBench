# TOOLS

- Listing service: search, inspect details, save candidates, check state, price, and viewing windows.
- Maps service: calculate door-to-door routes, walking segments, transit options, and detours.
- Calendar service: read conflicts and create internal reminders.
- Email service: read messages and save unsent drafts.
- Notion service: maintain candidate, budget, risk, authorization, and final-archive records.
- Review service: check community, facilities, noise, and safety reviews.
- Legal-search service: find contract-risk sources for a checklist; do not issue a final legal conclusion.
- Notification service: read periodic updates and reminders.
- Logistics information is for planning and risk records only; never change an address early.
