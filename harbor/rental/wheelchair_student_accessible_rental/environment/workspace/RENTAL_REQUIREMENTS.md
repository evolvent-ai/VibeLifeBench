# RENTAL_REQUIREMENTS

Must-have：
- Find an accessible one-bedroom apartment or a private master bedroom in a shared apartment; the total commute to the laboratory must be no more than 35 minutes; the entrance, elevator, door width, and bathroom must be verifiable on site; monthly rent must not exceed 6800 yuan.
- Monthly rent must not exceed 6800 yuan; upfront funds tied up must not exceed 17000 yuan.
- Key risks must be verified in writing: false accessibility labels, excessively steep entrance ramps, elevator outages, items obstructing hallways, unknown door width, steps along the route, unclear renovation promises.

Nice-to-have: a lower floor, clearly specified door width, enough turning space in the bathroom, a property manager willing to confirm in writing, and a family-friendly viewing window.

Deal-breaker: private payment, unclear authorization, unclear fee terms, unverifiable accessibility facilities, or pressure to exceed authorized actions.
