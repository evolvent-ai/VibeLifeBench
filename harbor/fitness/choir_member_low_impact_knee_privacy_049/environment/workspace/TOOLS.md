# Tool Guide

- `calendar`: read and maintain the private calendar, training reminders, rehearsal conflicts, and reduced-load windows.
- `health_tracker`: read steps, sleep, weight trends, pain and RPE notes, and training completion; mark missing data only.
- `weather`: query Ginkgo Community weather, rainfall, slippery surfaces, heat, humidity, and air quality.
- `email`: read choir notices; do not send or reply with health information without confirmation.
- `notion`: maintain the private control center, stage progress, risks, authorization, weather, and final review.
