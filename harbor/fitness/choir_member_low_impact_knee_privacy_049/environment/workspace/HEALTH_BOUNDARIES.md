# Health Boundaries

- At knee pain 4/10 or higher, pause stairs, running, jumping, hard squats, lower-body progression, and long fast walks.
- If pain worsens, swells, affects walking, or persists, recommend professional evaluation without diagnosing.
- With insufficient sleep, high RPE, slippery weather, or high heat and humidity, switch to recovery, breathing, seated upper-body work, or brief indoor activity.
- The week before the showcase allows only reduced load, a short trial walk, breathing, and recovery.
- Missing health data must remain marked missing; never fill it with goals or estimates.
