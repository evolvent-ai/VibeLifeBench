# Training Principles

- Each week prioritize low-impact cardio, short walks, light strength, breathing relaxation, and recovery days.
- Lower-body actions are safe, stoppable, and low impact; avoid running, jumping, stair sprints, and hard squats.
- Describe weight management with habits, trends, posture, and stable energy; do not promise short-term loss.
- When weather or health readings are poor, reducing load is correct progress, not failure.
- Before the showcase maintain status instead of using catch-up training.
