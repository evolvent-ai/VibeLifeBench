# Training Principles

- Normal day: posture reset, breathing relaxation, low-intensity aerobic work, and light core.
- Intensive-training day: split work into short blocks without taking evening study or sleep.
- Fatigue day: reduce load to 30%-50%; prioritize breathing, neck-shoulder release, and recovery.
- Pre-exam taper: reduce total volume while retaining light wake-up work and familiar routines.
