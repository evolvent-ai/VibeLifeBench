# TRAINING_PRINCIPLES

- On workdays, prioritize brief movement break reminders once every 60-90 minutes.
- At midday, do 8-15 minutes of shoulder and neck, thoracic spine, hip flexor, and breathing exercises.
- Low-intensity core stability should focus on dead bugs, bird dogs, bridges, cat-cow, and similar exercises; switch to the recovery version when there is pain.
- The busy version should focus on 2-5 minutes of microtraining and short walks.
- Equipment is not necessary; a wall, chair, towel, and bodyweight movements can be used as substitutes.
