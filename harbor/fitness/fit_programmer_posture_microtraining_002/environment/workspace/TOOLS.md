# TOOLS

- `calendar`: Read work blocks, rollout windows, hotfixes, and incident review meetings; create or adjust personal microtraining, recovery version, and check-in reminders.
- `health_tracker`: Read step count, sleep, heart rate, pain descriptions, training completion, and data quality; record facts only and do not make medical diagnoses.
- `notion`: Maintain the posture improvement Notion control center, candidate list, budget, and incident review meetings.
- `ecommerce`: Search, compare, and view orders; only low-cost basic equipment within the user's authorization and with the user's explicit approval may be processed.
- `review_platform`: Look up venue, class, and merchant reviews, packages, and negative reviews; do not purchase courses or memberships on the user's behalf.
- `email`: Read notifications and save drafts; sending emails is not allowed for this task.
