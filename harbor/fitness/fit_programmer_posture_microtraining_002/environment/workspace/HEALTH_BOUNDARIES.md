# HEALTH_BOUNDARIES

- Record pain using a 0-10 self-assessment.
- When neck and shoulder tension or lower-back soreness reaches 4/10 or above, the relevant training must be reduced or paused.
- When lower-back soreness worsens, do not schedule deadlifts, kettlebell swings, heavy squats, high-impact jumps, or challenging core exercises.
- When sleep is below 5 hours or there is accumulated sleep debt, do only recovery, light activity, breathing, short walks, or rest.
- A physician or physical therapist may be recommended for evaluation, but no diagnosis or treatment promises may be given.
