[2026-05-28 09:00 +08:00] Fee notice from Xihu District People's Court of Hangzhou:

For a property case, the acceptance fee is paid in progressive bands based on the requested amount. The plaintiff prepays it at filing and the losing party bears it after judgment. An asset-restraint application has a separate fee based on the property amount and requires security under the law. Use the court charge schedule and the court's payment notice for the exact standard.
